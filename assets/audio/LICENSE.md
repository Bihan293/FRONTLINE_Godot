# FRONTLINE — audio licenses

All sound effects in this folder are **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**.

## Gunshot

- `shot.wav` — derived from **"Gunshot sound effect"** by **Tabasco**
  - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sound-effect
  - License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  - Processing: converted to mono 44.1 kHz, gain-normalised, 0.2 s fade-out.

## Impacts, footsteps and interface

- `hit_flesh.ogg`, `hit_metal.ogg`, `empty.ogg`, `ui_click.ogg`,
  `reload_out.ogg`, `reload_in.wav`, `kill.ogg`, `footstep_000.ogg`,
  `footstep_001.ogg`, `footstep_002.ogg`
  - Derived from **Kenney — "Impact Sounds"** and **"Interface Sounds"**
  - Sources: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
    and https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  - License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  - Processing: `reload_in.wav` is a mix of a metal impact and a weapon
    switch, normalised to mono 44.1 kHz. Other files are used as shipped.
